Before you start

  1. Tone: Jobify speaks to job seekers plainly and warmly — never formal-officialese, never slangy. Everyday spoken Hindi, the register of a helpful colleague. Common English loanwords that Hindi speakers actually say (resume, profile, interview) are fine to keep.
  2. Blue chips like {count} are live values the app fills in — a name, a number, a date. Move them anywhere the Hindi sentence needs, but keep the spelling exactly and never delete or invent one. A changed chip crashes that screen.
  3. Amber-edged rows are still in English. Several are deliberate (a brand name, a currency symbol). Others are city names that do have common Devanagari spellings. Please confirm each one either way.
  4. To send corrections: print this page — each row gets a ruled box for handwritten notes — or reply with the grey keyName above any string and your suggested wording.
